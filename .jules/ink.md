# Ink's Manuscript

## 2024-05-22 - Standardized Micro-Typography
**Legibility:** Consistent sizing for micro-labels and badges across the application.
**Typesetting:** Introduced `text-xxs` utility (0.625rem / 10px) in `tailwind.config.js` to replace hardcoded `text-[10px]` values. This ensures that even the smallest text adheres to the design system and can be globally adjusted if needed.
